About Us

Committed to Your Health & Well-being

Established in 2006, Vivek Multispeciality Hospital has been a beacon of hope and healing in Varanasi. We combine state-of-the-art medical technology with compassionate care to provide comprehensive treatments across various specialties.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, high-quality, and advanced healthcare services to all sections of society with utmost ethical standards.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted healthcare institution in Purvanchal, known for clinical excellence, patient-centricity, and affordability.

Special Privilege Program

Golden Relationship Health Card

Vivek Multispeciality Hospital offers a special privilege program designed to provide comprehensive healthcare benefits and peace of mind for you and your entire family.

1. Free Medical Consultation

All cardholders and their family members receive free consultation by experienced doctors for a period of one entire year.

2. Special Discounts

Cardholders are eligible for special discounts on medicines, diagnostic tests, investigations, and other selected hospital services.

3. Hospitalization Benefits

If a cardholder or family member requires admission (IPD), special discounts are provided on treatment expenses and surgery concessions.
